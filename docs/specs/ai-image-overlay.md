**Tags:** `chrome-extension`, `visual-qa`, `google-images`, `accessibility`

## Goal

Make likely AI-generated images unmistakable without hiding confidence evidence, and verify the result on a Google Images search for real people.

## Scope

Content-script presentation and browser verification only; the detector model and 65 percent AI threshold remain unchanged.

## Non-Goals

- Changing the classifier, calibration, or confidence threshold
- Blurring real or uncertain images

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An image with an AI verdict is visibly blurred
2. The AI label is centered over the affected image and includes its confidence
3. Real and uncertain verdicts do not blur their images
4. The label exposes an accessible verdict and confidence name
5. A Google Images search for real person is exercised with the extension and its observed verdicts are recorded
